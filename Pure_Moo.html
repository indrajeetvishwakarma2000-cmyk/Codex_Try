BRAND IDENTITY / PURE MOO

PURE
MOO

A warm, friendly cow milk brand identity designed to feel as wholesome and approachable as the product itself.

The idea

Make everyday milk feel playful, pure and easy to trust through a soft visual world.

Identity direction

Comforting cream tones, sky blue typography and joyful cow-inspired forms bring the personality to life.

Brand touchpoints

From packaging to social content, the system is built to stay recognisable in every format.

Role

Brand strategy, visual identity, art direction and graphic design.
